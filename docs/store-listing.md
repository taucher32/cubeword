## English (en-US)

### App name (26)

```
Cubeword: Word Puzzle Game
```

### Short description (69)

```
Rotate cubes, find the letters, build the word! A clever word puzzle.
```

### Full description

```
Cubeword is a fresh word puzzle game where you rotate cubes to find letters and complete the hidden word!

🧊 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the cubes in the top row to rotate them, every face shows a different letter.
• Found the right letter? Drop the cube into the bottom row.
• Line up your letters, submit your word and rack up points!
• Find the hidden target word to finish the round and start a new one.

🧠 THINK AHEAD
Every cube has a limited number of rotations, so plan your moves! As you progress, words get longer, rotations get scarcer and the challenge keeps growing.

⭐ FEATURES
• Combo system: Chain valid words to multiply your score ×2 and ×3!
• Joker cube: Build your combo to earn joker cubes that arrive with the right letter.
• Hint cube: Every third round, a hint cube reveals the right letter in a single tap.
• Bonus rotations: Stuck? Watch a rewarded ad and get +3 rotations.
• High score tracking: Beat your best score and challenge yourself.
• Two languages: Play in English or Turkish and grow your vocabulary in both.
• Easy to hard: Start with short words and work your way up to tough long ones.

🎯 WHO IS IT FOR?
Anyone who loves word games, puzzles, brain teasers and quick mental workouts. Perfect for a few rounds on the bus, during a coffee break or before bed.

Ready to start rotating? Download Cubeword now and prove your word skills!
```
